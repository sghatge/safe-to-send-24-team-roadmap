Safe to Send · Ep 24 — The team average that identifies three people

You run an AI readiness survey across three teams. One of them, Analytics, has three people.

Report "Analytics: average 74" and you've just told everyone what those three people answered.

This repo turns anonymous readiness results into a 90-day plan, with one rule I'd make non-negotiable: any group under five is never reported on its own.

Then it does the part managers actually need:
→ finds the three biggest team gaps
→ moves common risk flags to the front of the queue
→ maps each gap to a concrete action for days 1–30, 31–60 and 61–90

In the synthetic example, the most enthusiastic team (Marketing) is also the one carrying the data-handling flags. So the plan starts with data privacy, not training.

A readiness score is only useful if it tells a manager what to do on Monday, without exposing anyone on Tuesday.
